Diagnostic & Troubleshooting Guide

Even with calibrated weights, batch generations can exhibit geometry drift or edge artifacts. Run this diagnostic checklist before exporting assets to your rigging or layout team:

  • Issue — Character generates with human hands instead of furred paws: Explicitly include "furred anthropomorphic hands with paw pads and short claws" and add "human skin fingers" to negative prompts.
  • Issue — Boots or pants cover and distort digitigrade ankle joints: Prompt for "open-heel leg wraps" or "digitigrade-fitted greaves exposing paw toes" so the hock joint remains anatomically clear.
  • Issue — Tall ears get clipped at the top of the frame: Add "full ear tips visible with generous headroom margin, centered full-body framing".

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I generate bipedal anthropomorphic characters instead of four-legged animals?

Always lead your prompt with "anthropomorphic bipedal character, standing upright on two legs, wearing clothing" and specify paw and leg posture.

What is the difference between digitigrade and plantigrade legs in furry art?

Digitigrade characters walk on their toes with an elevated ankle hock resembling canine or feline hind legs, whereas plantigrade characters stand flat on their soles like humans.

Can I generate a full fursona reference sheet with color swatches?

Yes. Combine front and back full-body views with a 5-color palette bar and close-up callouts for paw pads, eye color, and tail markings.
