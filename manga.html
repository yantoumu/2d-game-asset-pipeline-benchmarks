Diagnostic & Troubleshooting Guide

Even with calibrated weights, batch generations can exhibit geometry drift or edge artifacts. Run this diagnostic checklist before exporting assets to your rigging or layout team:

  • Issue — Output looks like a greyscale photograph instead of inked manga: Add "pure black and white ink, binary halftone screentone, no soft greyscale gradients, Japanese tankobon print style".
  • Issue — Panel borders merge into the artwork without clear gutters: Specify "distinct white panel gutters, crisp black rectangular panel frames".
  • Issue — AI-generated speech bubbles contain garbled pseudo-Japanese characters: Include "no text, empty white speech bubbles" or "clean illustration without text overlays" and letter dialogue in post.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I generate authentic black-and-white manga instead of greyscale shading?

Prompt specifically for "monochrome Japanese manga, G-pen ink lineart, halftone screentone dots, high-contrast beta black shadows" while excluding soft airbrush gradients.

Can I turn a photo into a manga background or character panel?

Yes. Reference-guided manga conversion extracts architectural perspective and pose contours from your photo and re-renders them with crisp ink lines and halftone screentones.

What aspect ratio is best for full manga pages?

Use a 2:3 or 5:7 vertical portrait aspect ratio, which closely matches standard Japanese B4/B6 tankobon manuscript dimensions.
