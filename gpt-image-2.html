Diagnostic & Troubleshooting Guide

Even with calibrated weights, batch generations can exhibit geometry drift or edge artifacts. Run this diagnostic checklist before exporting assets to your rigging or layout team:

  • Issue — Double letters (such as "LL" or "EE") merge or gain a third letter: Use ALL CAPS inside the quotation marks and specify "wide letter kerning, clean geometric sans-serif typography".
  • Issue — Secondary subtitle text turns into unreadable micro-squiggles: Limit subtitles to 3–4 words maximum and request "large legible subheading text" rather than paragraphs of fine print.
  • Issue — Text floats in mid-air instead of following the object surface: Anchor the physical medium ("screen-printed directly onto the cotton tote bag fabric").

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make an AI image generator spell text accurately?

Place the exact target words inside double quotation marks, keep each line under 5–6 words, use uppercase lettering for complex words, and specify a clear font style such as bold sans-serif or vintage serif.

Can GPT-Image-2 handle multi-line text layouts on posters and book covers?

Yes. By explicitly assigning spatial zones—such as a main title at the top in bold serif and a subtitle at the bottom in clean sans-serif—you can render structured multi-line poster typography.

Why does cursive or script lettering sometimes misspell words in AI images?

Connected cursive strokes blur character boundaries in latent space; switching to bold block serif, geometric sans-serif, or stencil lettering dramatically increases character-level spelling accuracy.
